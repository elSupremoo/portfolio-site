Computer Vision Oct 2026

Sanjay

Street-level image geolocation for Nepal. Locates a street-level photo anywhere in Nepal from the image alone.

  • Within 1 km 58.4% of the time from one view, and 74.6% from four views (median error 0.52 km and 0.29 km). Tested on 3,000 held-out locations.
  • Fine-tuned CLIP ViT-B/16 with a hybrid loss: geocell classification plus contrastive (InfoNCE) learning with hard negatives. This doubled within-1-km retrieval from 26% to 53%.
  • GPU retrieval index of 2.9 million fingerprints (356,890 locations × 8 headings), built for the whole country in about 3 hours on one 12 GB GPU.
  • Four-view fusion halves gross failures (errors over 50 km drop from 12.3% to 5.8%). Color augmentation raised accuracy under a warm color cast from 15% to 49%.

Computer Vision Nov 2026

geo-trust

Can you trust a geolocation model? A follow-up to Sanjay that stress-tests CLIP geolocation on the public OpenStreetView-5M benchmark (10,000 test images, 195 countries).

  • Robustness: heavy JPEG compression costs 41 points of accuracy, and privacy blurring costs 36.
  • Knowing when to abstain: answering only the most confident 10% cuts the error rate from 59.5% to 18.5%.
  • Conformal prediction sets hit 90.0% coverage on clean data. Under JPEG shift, retrieval coverage collapses to 34% (a "hubness" failure), and corruption-aware calibration restores it to 77%.
PyTorch open_clip OpenCV

Machine Learning Nov 2026

fedpriv-bench

Federated and private attack detection, done carefully. Re-examines my own internship result on public power-grid data, with leakage-safe splits and a compute-matched baseline.

  • Showed that splitting random rows instead of whole scenarios inflates a model's score from 0.60 to 0.92 macro-F1.
  • At equal compute, federated learning matches centralized training rather than beating it (0.546 vs 0.530 macro-F1). Uneven data across clients drops it to 0.414.
  • Differential privacy (DP-SGD) hits rare classes hardest: at ε ≤ 8, rarest-class recall falls from 28% to 6-9%.
  • A 70-run, resumable experiment sweep in which every table and figure is regenerated from saved results. 14 tests.
PyTorch Opacus scikit-learn
